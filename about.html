Our Story

Jeevan Ankur Seva Foundation was born from a deep, unwavering desire to provide a safe haven, dignity, and love to abandoned and solitary elderly individuals. Rooted firmly in the profound Indian cultural values of "Matru Devo Bhava, Pitru Devo Bhava" (Revere your Mother as God, Revere your Father as God), our founders recognized the growing need for compassionate elder care in our society.

We witnessed the heart-wrenching reality of senior citizens left to fend for themselves, struggling with loneliness, poor health, and the absence of a loving family. Jeevan Ankur was established not merely as a shelter, but as a vibrant, loving home where our elders can spend their twilight years with respect, emotional support, and absolute dignity.

Today, our Vridhashram stands as a testament to the power of community, empathy, and dedicated service, blossoming into a sanctuary where every resident is treated as a cherished family member.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

To provide exceptional care, boundless love, absolute dignity, and a safe, family-like environment for senior citizens. We are especially dedicated to supporting those who are solitary, destitute, or in need of specialized attention, ensuring their physical, mental, and emotional well-being.

Our Vision

To cultivate and inspire a society where every elderly person lives their golden years with utmost dignity, profound respect, and complete emotional fulfillment, fully integrated and cherished by the community they helped build.

Our Core Values

Dignity

Honoring the life experiences and inherent worth of every senior citizen.

Compassion

Delivering care with genuine love, empathy, and understanding.

Transparency

Maintaining open, honest operations and clear communication with our supporters.

Community

Fostering a sense of belonging and familial connection among residents and staff.

Journey of Care

2018

The Foundation

Jeevan Ankur Seva Foundation was formally registered and established with a vision to create a safe haven for destitute elders.

2019

Welcoming Our First Residents

We officially opened the doors of our Vridhashram in Kurla East, welcoming our first group of senior citizens into their new home.

2021

Community Outreach Launch

Initiated regular health camps and community support programs for elderly individuals residing outside our immediate facility.

2022

Facility Expansion

Upgraded our living spaces and medical care facilities to accommodate more residents and provide specialized healthcare support.

Present

Growing Family

Our volunteer network has grown significantly, and we continue to nurture a thriving, loving family environment for all our revered residents.
