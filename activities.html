A Day in the Life

Our structured routine blends physical wellness, mental engagement, and spiritual peace, ensuring our residents stay active and joyful.

Morning Yoga & Meditation

Gentle stretching and breathing exercises led by experienced instructors to start the day with peace and vitality.

Art & Craft Sessions

Creative expressions through painting, drawing, and simple crafts to enhance fine motor skills and imagination.

Music & Bhajan Evenings

Soulful evening gatherings for singing traditional bhajans and kirtans, bringing immense joy and spiritual solace.

Board Games & Cards

Friendly matches of chess, carrom, and card games to promote mental agility and healthy social interaction.

Storytelling Circles

Sharing lifetime experiences and reciting tales that foster deep connections and intergenerational wisdom sharing.

Garden & Nature Time

Enjoying fresh air with supervised garden walks and light gardening to keep in touch with nature's therapeutic benefits.

Festival Celebrations

We celebrate every major festival with zeal, ensuring our elders feel the warmth and festivity of a large, loving family.

Diwali - Festival of Lights

Decorating with diyas, making vibrant rangolis, and sharing traditional sweets.

Holi

Joyful celebration with organic colors, music, and festive delicacies.

Raksha Bandhan

Tying rakhis to symbolize love, protection, and strong familial bonds.

Navratri & Garba

Devotional songs, aarti, and gentle garba to honor the divine feminine.

National Days

Patriotic fervor with flag hoisting on Independence and Republic Day.

Birthdays

Cake cutting, heartfelt gifts, and showering blessings on our residents' special days.

Welcoming Volunteers & Community

The heart of Jeevan Ankur is deeply connected to our generous community. We frequently host volunteers from schools, colleges, and corporate organizations who spend quality time with our seniors.

  • Companionship Programs Youth and adults spend afternoons simply talking, reading, or listening to the elders.
  • Special Meal Sponsorships Families often celebrate their own special occasions by sponsoring and serving special meals.
  • Entertainment Sessions Local artists and groups perform music, drama, and comedy to entertain our residents.
  • Health Camps Medical professionals and corporate CSR teams regularly organize specialized health check-ups.
