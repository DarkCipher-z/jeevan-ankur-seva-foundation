Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries about our foundation.

You can visit us from Monday to Saturday (10 AM - 5 PM) and Sunday (10 AM - 1 PM) at our Kurla East facility. We highly recommend calling us at +91 8169615400 prior to your visit so we can ensure the timing doesn't interrupt the residents' meals or resting schedules.

We accept donations via UPI, Bank Transfer, and Cheques. You can also provide in-kind donations like groceries, medicines, and daily essentials. Please visit our Donate page for detailed banking and UPI information.

Yes, absolutely! We always welcome volunteers who want to spend quality time with our elders, help organize events, or provide medical checkups. You can fill out the volunteer application on our Donate page or contact us directly to discuss how you can help.

For admissions, please use the contact form above and select "Admission Inquiry" as the subject, or call our helpline directly. We will schedule an initial meeting to discuss the elder's medical history, required care levels, and our admission protocols.

Yes, all financial contributions to Jeevan Ankur Seva Foundation are eligible for tax deductions under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act. We will provide you with an official receipt carrying our 80G registration number upon successful donation.
