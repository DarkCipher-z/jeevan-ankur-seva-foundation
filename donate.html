Every Contribution Makes a Difference

At Jeevan Ankur Seva Foundation, we believe that every elderly person deserves to live their twilight years with respect, love, and a sense of belonging. Many of our residents come to us after facing abandonment or severe hardship. Your contribution helps us provide them with more than just a shelter; it provides them with a warm, family-like home.

When you donate, you are directly funding nutritious meals, vital healthcare and medicines, comfortable living arrangements, and recreational activities that keep our elders joyful and engaged.

80G Tax Exemption Benefits

Your contributions to Jeevan Ankur Seva Foundation are eligible for tax deductions under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

Upon successful donation, donors will receive an official 80G receipt which can be used to claim tax benefits during income tax filing. The foundation's 80G registration details and PAN will be provided upon request and printed on your official receipt.
