A Warm Welcome to Our Home

Located in the heart of Kurla East, Mumbai, the Jeevan Ankur Vridhashram is thoughtfully designed to provide a serene, safe, and comfortable living space for our elders. Our facility isn't just a building; it is a vibrant community filled with laughter, shared stories, and dedicated care.

Our Location

67 D Bungalow (behind Madhav Baugh Clinic), Kamghar Nagar,
beside Shreeram Co-operative Society, Sidheswar Building,
Kurla East, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400024.

Our Living Spaces

Shared Rooms

Comfortable, well-ventilated multi-bed rooms designed for restful sleep, complete with individual storage spaces and easily accessible amenities.

Common Areas

Inviting spaces including a TV lounge, a serene prayer room for spiritual reflection, and an accessible garden for relaxing walks.

Dining Hall

A spacious communal dining area where residents gather to enjoy nutritious, freshly prepared vegetarian meals together as a family.
