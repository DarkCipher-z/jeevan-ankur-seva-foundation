A Holistic Approach to Elderly Care

At Jeevan Ankur Seva Foundation, we believe that true care goes beyond meeting physical needs. We provide a family-like atmosphere that nurtures emotional well-being, ensures dignity, and fosters a sense of community among our residents. Our services are designed to address every aspect of our seniors' lives, giving them peace of mind and joy in their golden years.

Residential Care & Shelter

We provide a safe, secure, and serene living environment designed keeping the comfort of senior citizens in mind. Our accommodations feel just like home, ensuring dignity and a peaceful stay.

  • Safe, clean, and well-maintained living spaces with comfortable bedding
  • Basic assistance and personal hygiene care tailored to individual needs
  • Regular laundry and housekeeping services to maintain cleanliness
  • Round-the-clock supervision and support from dedicated staff

Nutrition & Healthcare

Proper nutrition and regular healthcare monitoring are vital for our residents. We ensure they receive balanced meals and medical attention whenever necessary to maintain their health and vitality.

  • Three freshly prepared, balanced vegetarian meals daily, plus healthy snacks
  • Special dietary accommodations for conditions like diabetes or hypertension
  • Routine medical check-ups conducted by visiting doctors
  • Medication management, health monitoring, and emergency healthcare support

Emotional & Recreational Well-Being

Companionship and joy are the heart of our home. We keep our residents actively engaged physically and mentally, banishing loneliness and fostering a vibrant community spirit.

  • Daily social interaction and bonding through storytelling circles
  • Indoor games (chess, carrom, cards) and outdoor activities (garden walks)
  • Spiritual fulfillment through morning yoga and bhajan/kirtan sessions
  • Grand celebrations for birthdays and major festivals (Diwali, Holi, Raksha Bandhan)

Community Outreach

Our mission extends beyond our walls. We actively work within the broader community to raise awareness about elder rights and build a society that respects and cherishes its senior citizens.

  • Elder rights awareness campaigns advocating for dignity and respect
  • Intergenerational bonding programs with local school and college students
  • Sensitization workshops focusing on proper elderly care and empathy
  • Community volunteer coordination for engaging and assisting our elders
